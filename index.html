NEXORA Terms & Intellectual Property Protection

Last Updated: October 2026 | Standard Version 3.4

1. Purpose & Confidentiality Shield

NEXORA is a premier venture ecosystem built on uncompromised confidentiality. When an entrepreneur submits a venture proposal or pitch documentation, all proprietary schematics, algorithms, and business plans remain under strict non-disclosure protection. Investors accessing restricted materials agree not to reverse-engineer, leak, or exploit any confidential ideas.

2. Intellectual Property Rights

Submission of any venture or document to NEXORA does not transfer any intellectual property rights to the platform or to visiting investors. All patents, copyrights, source code, and trade secrets remain 100% the sole property of the founder/entrepreneur.

3. Mandatory Accreditation & Role-Based Access

Investors undergo identity verification before unlocking confidential attachments. Entrepreneurs retain granular authority to toggle files between public summary and restricted access at any time through their Privacy Controls.

4. User Conduct & Safe Communication

All discussions through the NEXORA secure communication feature are encrypted. Solicitation of fraudulent investment or violation of international securities laws will lead to immediate account termination and legal enforcement.
